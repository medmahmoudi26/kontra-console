Four poll states, and the fourth is not a worse third. serving is a poll inside POLL_FRESH_MS (120 s). stale is an identity Temporal still lists — it keeps them about five minutes after a Worker stops, so the identity alone reports a dead Worker as healthy. undated is Temporal listing an identity without a timestamp, which pollIsFresh counts as neither. nothing polling is a queue with no identity at all — the Placement converged, and a dispatch to it will hang to StartToClose.
